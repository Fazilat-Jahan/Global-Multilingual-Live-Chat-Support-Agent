## What is your return window?

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long
as the item is unused and in its original packaging. Some categories, such as
personal care items and final-sale products, are not eligible for return.

## How do I start a return?

Log into your account, open the order you want to return, and select "Start a
Return." You will receive a prepaid return shipping label by email. Drop the
package off at any authorized carrier location.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?

Yes. During the return process you can choose to exchange the item for a
different size or color, subject to availability. If the replacement item costs
more, you will be charged the difference; if it costs less, the difference is
refunded.

## Who pays for return shipping?

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrectly shipped items. For
change-of-mind returns, a $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund
unless you are a loyalty program member.
