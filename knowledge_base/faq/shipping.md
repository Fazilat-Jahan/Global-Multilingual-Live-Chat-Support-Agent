## How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within the country takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping
takes 1 to 2 business days and is available at checkout for an additional fee.
International shipping typically takes 7 to 14 business days depending on the
destination country and customs processing times.

## How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is free on all orders over $50. Orders under $50 have a flat
shipping fee of $4.99. Express shipping costs an additional $12.99 regardless of
order size. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on
destination and package weight.

## Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order ships, you will receive an email with a tracking number and
a link to track your package in real time. You can also view tracking information
by logging into your account and viewing your order history.

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. Availability and delivery estimates are shown
at checkout before you complete your purchase. Some countries may have import
duties or taxes that are the responsibility of the customer.
