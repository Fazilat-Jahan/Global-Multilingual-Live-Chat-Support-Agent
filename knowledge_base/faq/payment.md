## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express,
Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. In select regions we also support
bank transfers and cash on delivery.

## Is it safe to enter my card details on your site?

Yes. All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways using
end-to-end encryption. We never store your full card number on our servers.

## Can I pay in installments?

Installment payments are available on orders over $100 through our "Pay in 4"
partner at checkout. Approval and terms depend on the payment provider's own
eligibility criteria.

## Why was my payment declined?

Payments are commonly declined due to insufficient funds, incorrect card
details, or your bank's fraud protection flagging the transaction. Please verify
your details and contact your bank if the issue persists.
