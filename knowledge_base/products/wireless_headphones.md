## Overview

The AeroSound Wireless Headphones deliver active noise cancellation, 30-hour
battery life, and Bluetooth 5.3 connectivity in a lightweight over-ear design.
They are available in black, white, and navy blue.

## Battery and Charging

A full charge takes approximately 2 hours and provides up to 30 hours of
playback with noise cancellation on, or 40 hours with it off. A 10-minute quick
charge provides about 4 hours of playback.

## Warranty

AeroSound Wireless Headphones come with a 1-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water
damage outside the IPX4 rating, or normal wear and tear.

## Compatibility

The headphones work with any Bluetooth-enabled device, including phones,
tablets, and laptops. A companion app is available for iOS and Android to
customize equalizer settings and noise cancellation levels.

## Price

The AeroSound Wireless Headphones are available for $149.99 USD. They can be purchased online or at select retail stores.
