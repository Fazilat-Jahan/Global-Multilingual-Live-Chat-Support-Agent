## Overview

The PulseFit Smart Watch tracks heart rate, sleep, steps, and blood oxygen
levels, with a 1.4-inch AMOLED display and up to 7 days of battery life on a
single charge. It is water resistant up to 50 meters.

## Health Tracking Features

PulseFit continuously monitors heart rate and sleep stages, and provides
weekly health reports through the companion app. It also includes guided
breathing exercises and stress-level tracking based on heart rate variability.

## Battery Life

Typical use provides up to 7 days of battery life; with always-on display
enabled, battery life is reduced to approximately 3 days. A full charge from
empty takes about 90 minutes using the included magnetic charging cable.

## Warranty

PulseFit Smart Watch includes a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects. Screen damage from drops and battery degradation from normal use are
not covered.
