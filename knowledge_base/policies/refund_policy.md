## Refund Eligibility

Refunds are issued for items returned within the 30-day return window in
resellable condition. Refunds are not issued for gift cards, downloadable
software, or items marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

## Refund Processing Time

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, refunds are processed within 3
to 5 business days. It may take an additional 5 to 10 business days for the
refund to appear on your original payment method, depending on your bank or
card issuer.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued if an item is returned used, damaged, or missing
original packaging, at the discretion of our quality inspection team.

## Refunds for Cancelled Orders

If an order is cancelled before it ships, a full refund is issued automatically
to the original payment method within 24 hours. Orders that have already shipped
must go through the standard return process instead.
